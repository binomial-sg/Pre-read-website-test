Prelude

A lesson from the 4x100m relay

Two teams, two approaches to the baton. Toggle between them to compare.

How did the best US runners fail to win the 4x100m relay in the past 20 years?

The US consistently fielded the fastest athletes in the world, who could run 100m in under 10s.

Yet, in the past 20 years, the team didn't win a relay medal. Tap a year:

Note: Tokyo's 2020 Olympics occurred in 2021 due to the COVID-19 pandemic. Source: Washington Post, NBC News, The Guardian.

"The USA team did everything wrong in the men's relay. The passing system is wrong, athletes running the wrong legs, and it was clear that there was no leadership."

Carl Lewis, American athlete, 9-time Olympic Gold medallist. Commenting on the 2021 Olympics.
Each runner focused on their run → Handoffs were deprioritised and practiced only once a year
Each department can be the best at what they do, but if they don't see themselves as part of an organisation, the organisation won't meet its goals.

Japan, the massive underdogs, perfected their handoffs and won Olympic Silver in 2016

  • The Japanese, in 2016, did not have a runner who could run 100m in under 10s individually
  • Everyone else focused on the run, while Japan focused on handoffs → Practiced for 6 months prior to the Olympics
  • Biomechanics were analysed to ensure the runner receiving the baton is efficiently positioned to burst forward
  • Set an Asian record of 37.6s 4x100m relay

Source: Reuters.

"The baton changes are always good and that's what always helps them, and I think they executed it well today, so I'm not shocked."

Usain Bolt, 8-time Olympic Gold medallist. Commenting on the 2016 Japanese relay performance.
Each Japanese runner handing over the baton took ownership to ensure the next runner was able to maximise his performance.

Overview

Project objectives

During our strategic planning workshop, we identified strategic initiatives to achieve our goal of becoming more efficient and effective. As part of that process, we wanted to focus on optimising core processes and personnel as well.

This project aims to:
1
Identify the biggest friction points and issues causing hold ups in production, and their root causes
2
Develop the best operating model which addresses these issues
3
Pilot the newly minted operating model with clear ownership and success metrics
